Pioneering the Future of STEM Education

Led by a team of passionate B.Tech students, Interstellar Robotics and Innovation Park brings hands-on STEM, robotics, and aerospace education directly to school students.

  • Hands-on, kit-based workshops
  • Early exposure to microcontrollers & sensors
  • Real-world engineering projects
  • Expert mentorship from industry professionals

OUR PROGRAMS

Module A: Autonomous Robotics

Classes 8–10

Learn microcontroller integration and basic logic programming through hands-on robotics projects.

  • Introduction to Arduino & microcontroller architecture
  • Interfacing sensors: Ultrasonic & Infrared
  • Basic programming logic: If/Else & loops

Hands-on Project:

Building a Line-Following or Obstacle-Avoiding Robot

Module B: Advanced STEM & Aerospace

Classes 11–12

Explore Internet of Things (IoT), coding, and flight physics with advanced aerospace concepts.

  • Python programming for hardware
  • Wireless communication: Bluetooth & Wi-Fi
  • Aerospace basics: Aerodynamics & drone mechanics

Hands-on Project:

Assembling a smart IoT automation system or a mini-quadcopter
